=== TITLE ===
Guardian - No Escapes and No Stuck Transport Boxes

=== BRIEF OVERVIEW (Nexus "summary", max 350 characters) ===
Animals keep escaping or end up sitting in transport boxes? Guardian switches escapes off, sends a keeper for any animal that still gets out, and unboxes animals left stuck in a box in their own habitat. Every part can be switched off, the wait times are adjustable, and a panel shows what it did.

=== DESCRIPTION (paste into the BBCode editor) ===
[size=5][b]Guardian[/b][/size]
[i]Stops escapes, brings escaped animals home, unboxes animals stuck in transport boxes.[/i]

Animals climbing out of habitats, guests running off, and animals sitting in transport boxes for no clear reason: Guardian handles all of it in the background. A small button and panel in the park show what it is doing.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Block escapes[/b] (on): switches animal escapes off. This is the same switch as the Sandbox option "Animal Escapes", applied in any park.
[*][b]Send escapees home[/b] (on): an escaped animal, or one left boxed outside its habitat, is brought back by a keeper.
[*][b]Unbox stuck animals[/b] (on): an animal left in a transport box inside its own habitat, with no move under way, is let out, just like pressing the game's Unbox button.
[*][b]Paid emergency capture[/b] (off): an animal that stays loose too long is caught with the game's Emergency Capture. This costs money, so it's off by default.
[*][b]Adjustable wait times[/b]: set with -/+ buttons, in real seconds, so game speed doesn't change them.
[*][b]Results[/b]: counts for this session and all time: unboxed, sent home, back in habitat, emergency captured.
[*][b]Diagnose[/b]: one click shows the details of every escaped or boxed animal, handy for bug reports.
[*][b]In the Esc menu[/b]: a "Mods" box next to the game's menu opens the panel. The screen button can be switched off if you'd rather not see it.
[*][b]Size[/b]: make the panel and button smaller or bigger (50-200 %). They also follow your screen resolution and the game's UI scale.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Default wait times[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Escaped, before a keeper is sent: 15 s
[*]Boxed outside its habitat, before it is sent home: 30 s
[*]Boxed in its own habitat, before it is unboxed: 10 s
[*]Between two tries for the same animal: 45 s
[*]Escaped, before the paid emergency capture: 120 s
[/list]
The wait gives the game's own staff the first chance, and a box you made on purpose stays shut for a while.

[size=4][b]What it leaves alone[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Animals already being moved (by a keeper, the vet, you or another mod).
[*]Animals being carried, or in the Trade Centre, quarantine or the vet surgery.
[*]Animals a vet has tranquillised.
[*]Everything while the game is paused.
[*]An animal the game keeps putting back in its box: in Franchise zoos, after 3 tries it is left alone for 2 minutes (the panel says why). In Career and Sandbox Guardian keeps unboxing it, and after 3 quick reboxes asks a keeper to carry it to a better spot.
[*]A box in a broken habitat (open gate or broken barrier) stays shut, so the animal doesn't walk straight out again. Repair the habitat first.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Why animals escape[/b][/size]
Climbing animals get over walls that aren't climb-proof, any animal crosses a null barrier with nothing natural on top, and worn-out barriers break (Fence Dilapidation). The vet then tranquillises the escapee and puts it in a transport box for the trip home, which is where most "random" boxes come from.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]In Steam: right-click Planet Zoo 2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Copy the [b]Win64[/b] folder from the zip into that game folder and choose "Replace" / merge. Nothing of the game is overwritten; only a new PZ2Guardian folder is added.
[*]Check that [i]Planet Zoo 2\Win64\ovldata\PZ2Guardian\Main.ovl[/i] exists.
[*]Start the game. The one-time "modifications detected" notice is normal.
[/list]
[b]Uninstall:[/b] delete [i]Planet Zoo 2\Win64\ovldata\PZ2Guardian[/i].
[b]Update:[/b] copy the new Win64 folder over the old one.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Works alongside [b]Mike's QoL Toolbox[/b] (formerly Genes & Gold, PZ2QoL): both share the in-park screen. Works alongside [b]AutoHub[/b]: both share the in-park screen and the "Mods" box in the Esc menu. No game files are changed. The only thing written is Guardian's own small entry in your player profile (its settings); nothing goes into park saves or online saves.
